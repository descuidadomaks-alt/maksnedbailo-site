# Care Less — UK Locksmith Missed-Calls: Creative Package

## Video 1 — "The call you didn't hear" (15–30s)

**Shot-by-shot (screen recording, no founder on camera):**
1. (0–4s) Screen-record a phone lock screen with 3 missed-call notifications stacking up in real time (staged — record on a test phone with calls placed from a second device).
2. (4–8s) Cut to the messaging app: an SMS auto-reply fires and appears on screen, timestamped seconds after the missed call.
3. (8–15s) Cut to a calendar app: a new booking appears with caller name/job details attached.
4. (15–20s, optional if running the full 30s) End card: brand wordmark + "£167/mo pilot" + booking link.

**VO script (verbatim):**
> "Three missed calls. That's not bad luck — that's three jobs another locksmith just picked up. This is what happens the moment you miss one instead: [SMS auto-reply appears] answered in under 60 seconds, job booked straight into your diary. No answering service. No message-taking. Just your calls, answered, every time."

**ElevenLabs settings:** UK male voice, "Conversational" style preset, stability ~40–50% (some natural variation, not flat), similarity boost ~75%, speed at or just under 1.0x (unhurried, trustworthy — not a hard-sell read).

## Video 2 — "Your diary, filling itself" (15–30s)

**Shot-by-shot:**
1. (0–5s) Screen-record a van dashboard or phone-in-pocket moment (staged, generic — no real customer footage) implying "mid-job."
2. (5–12s) Cut to a calendar app receiving a new booking notification live, with caller details visible.
3. (12–18s) Zoom on the booking card: name, job type, time slot.
4. (18–20s, optional) End card: brand wordmark + pricing + booking link.

**VO script (verbatim):**
> "You're mid-job, phone's in the van. Somebody's locked out three streets away. Here's what happens now: they call, get answered instantly, get asked the right questions, and land straight in your diary — booked before you've even finished the job you're on."

**ElevenLabs settings:** same voice/preset as Video 1 for consistency across the campaign.
