## SMS — within 60 seconds of the Instant Form lead

> Hi [Name], it's Maks from Care Less — thanks for your interest in never missing a call again. Quick one: what's your busiest time of week for missed calls? Reply here and I'll call you within the hour.

## Call opener (verbatim)

> Hi [Name], it's Maks from Care Less — you just told us you're missing calls most weeks as a locksmith. Can I ask, when that happens, do you know if that customer calls someone else, or waits for you?

Let them answer. Whatever they say, it opens straight into the offer: every missed call answered in under 60 seconds and booked into their diary, or that month's free.

## 3-touch no-reply sequence

Send only if the lead hasn't replied to the initial SMS. Stop the sequence the moment they reply, at any point.

**Day 1, PM (evening of the same day, if no reply to the morning/immediate SMS):**

> [Name], following up in case that first text got buried under a job. Still keen to stop losing calls to voicemail? One question and I'll call you: roughly how many calls a week do you reckon you're missing right now?

**Day 3:**

> [Name], last thing before I leave this — most locksmiths we talk to are losing at least one job a week to a call they never got back to. If that's not you, no worries, just reply "not now" and I'll leave it. If it is, reply "yes" and I'll ring you today.

**Day 7 (final):**

> [Name], closing this one out — I won't chase after today. If missed calls start costing you jobs again, the link's always open: https://zcal.co/carelessmaks/ai-map. Good luck out there.
